# Word Ribbon icon artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for Batch Citations 1.1.2. The original transparent PNGs are retained here; the Word assembly embeds them and renders their alpha bounds into the native 32px icon box. The existing 16px menu glyphs are drawn at native size. No external image files are required by the installed add-in.

Shared prompt: A polished isolated Windows Microsoft Word Ribbon icon for a Zotero companion add-in. Genuinely transparent background, fine cool-grey outlines, restrained blue accents, balanced white space, professional small-size legibility, front-facing vector-like finish. No captions, decorative backgrounds, 3D, shadows or gloss. Intended for a 32px toolbar with safe transparent edge padding.

Batch prompt: Four bracketed numbers [1], [2], [3], [4] in a regular compact 2x2 grid. Equal cells and spacing. Numerals optically centered horizontally and vertically. In particular, center the vertical stem of 1 in its cell, with a modest centered base. Neutral dark-grey brackets and calm medium-blue numerals; no surrounding paper or extra symbols.

Display prompt: A refined native document with folded upper-right corner, containing an elegant blue X with raised 2 (X²). Both glyphs fully inside the paper, with safe margins from the right edge and fold. White paper and delicate cool-grey outline. Center the combined mark in the usable interior, large enough for 32px legibility; no other text or meaningless lines.

The final preview and OLE pixel checks are recorded in docs/test-results. Visual centering is also inspected at native 32px size; numeric bounds alone are not treated as visual approval.
